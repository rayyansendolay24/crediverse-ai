Riverside edges Lakeview 3-2 in overtime to claim district final

Riverside High School won its first district championship in eleven years on Saturday, beating Lakeview 3-2 in overtime at Memorial Stadium in front of an estimated 2,000 fans.

Junior forward Priya Nair scored the game-winning goal four minutes into overtime, redirecting a corner kick from midfielder Jalen Brooks past Lakeview's goalkeeper. It was Nair's second goal of the match; she also opened the scoring in the 18th minute.

Lakeview, the two-time defending champion, twice fought back from a goal down, with senior captain Sofia Delgado equalizing in the 61st minute to force extra time. Lakeview coach Dennis Hart said afterward that fatigue from a condensed playoff schedule played a role in the loss.

"We played three games in six days. I'm proud of how hard this group fought, but you could see legs going in the second half," Hart said.

Riverside coach Angela Vance, now in her third season, said the win validated a rebuilding effort that began after the team missed the playoffs entirely in 2023.

"Two years ago we didn't even qualify. This group bought into a different style of play, more possession-based, and it paid off tonight," Vance said.

Riverside advances to the regional semifinal next Saturday against Northbrook, which won its district final 4-0 earlier in the day.
